5. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with the following categories of third parties:

  • Affiliate networks & operators — click and referral data is shared with partner betting sites to verify conversions and process commission payments.
  • Analytics providers — we use Google Analytics (with IP anonymisation enabled) to understand site traffic.
  • Hosting providers — our website host processes server log data in the course of providing hosting services.
  • Legal authorities — we may disclose data if required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights.

7. Data Retention

  • Analytics data: retained for 14 months, then automatically deleted by Google Analytics.
  • Affiliate click data: retained for up to 3 years for commission audit purposes.
  • Contact enquiries: retained for 2 years from the date of last correspondence.
  • Server logs: retained for up to 90 days.

9. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers (including Google Analytics) may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this occurs, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, to protect your data to EEA standards.

10. Children

BetWise is strictly an 18+ website. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at privacy@betwise.com and we will delete it promptly.
