How Free Bets Work

A free bet is a token that lets you place a wager without using your own money. If the bet wins, you receive the winnings but not the stake. For example, a £10 free bet on a 3/1 winner returns £30 in winnings — not £40. If the bet loses, you lose nothing from your own balance.

⚠️ Watch out for these common mistakes:

Not meeting the minimum odds on your qualifying bet · Placing the qualifying bet on a market excluded from the offer · Letting free bets expire before using them · Using a VPN which may void your bonus · Depositing via a payment method excluded from the offer (e.g. PayPal at some operators).

Common Free Bet Terms Explained

  • Minimum odds: Your qualifying bet must be at or above these odds. bet365 requires 1/5 (1.20); William Hill requires evens (2.00).
  • Expiry date: Free bets expire — usually within 7 days. Set a reminder.
  • Non-withdrawable stake: When you win with a free bet, you receive only the winnings — the stake is not returned.
  • Qualifying bet: The initial bet you must place to trigger the free bet offer.
  • Excluded markets: Some markets (e.g. Bet Builder, virtuals) do not qualify. Check the T&Cs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I withdraw a free bet?
No — free bets cannot be withdrawn directly. You must use them to place a bet first. If the bet wins, you can withdraw the winnings — but not the free bet stake itself.
What happens if my free bet selection is voided?
If your free bet selection is voided, the free bet token is usually returned to your account to use again. Check the specific operator's terms to confirm, as policies vary.
Which betting site has the best free bet in 2026?
William Hill offers the best free bet for UK bettors in 2026 — Bet £10 Get £40 using code G40. For US bettors, FanDuel offers Bet $5 Get $150 regardless of whether the qualifying bet wins or loses.
Can I use multiple free bets at the same time?
This depends on the operator. Most allow one free bet per bet slip. However, if you have multiple free bet tokens, you can usually use them on separate bets. Check the operator's specific terms.
