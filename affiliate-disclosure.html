Last updated: May 6, 2026  ·  Effective immediately
Summary (Plain English)

BetWise earns money when you click our links and sign up at a betting site. This never affects our reviews — we only recommend licensed, reputable operators. You never pay more because of our links.

1. Who We Are

BetWise ("we", "us", "our") is an independent gambling comparison and review website. We are not a betting operator and we do not accept bets. Our sole purpose is to help users find the best-rated, licensed betting sites and casinos available in their country.

2. How We Make Money

BetWise participates in affiliate marketing programs operated by licensed gambling companies. This means that when you click a link on our website and subsequently create an account or make a deposit at a partner site, we may receive a commission. There are two main types of commission we receive:

  • Revenue Share (RevShare): We receive a percentage of the net revenue generated by players we refer, typically between 25–45%.
  • Cost Per Acquisition (CPA): We receive a fixed fee for each qualifying new player we refer, typically between €30–€150 per player.
  • Hybrid: A combination of both RevShare and CPA.

You will never be charged any additional fees or receive worse terms as a result of using our affiliate links. In many cases, our links unlock exclusive bonuses not available to direct sign-ups.
